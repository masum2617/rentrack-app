# House Rent Management

The language used to describe a landlord's rental records and shared electricity usage.

## Language

**Landlord**:
The owner who rents accommodation to tenants and keeps records of their charges and payments.
_Avoid_: Admin when referring to ownership

**Tenant**:
A person who rents accommodation from the landlord. Their identity and rental history remain part of the records after they leave.
_Avoid_: User when referring to the renting person

**Property**:
A house or building at a location containing one or more rental units.
_Avoid_: Rental unit when referring to the whole property

**Rental unit**:
An accommodation, such as a flat, rented as a single unit within a property.
_Avoid_: Property when referring to one flat within a building

**Tenancy**:
The rental arrangement linking a tenant to a rental unit for a period, including its agreed rent and advance terms. Ending the arrangement preserves its history.
_Avoid_: Tenant when referring to the rental arrangement

**Notice-period advance**:
Money collected at the beginning of a tenancy and held to cover rent during the departure notice period. The original monetary credit stays unchanged after a rent increase, and its initial amount can vary with the landlord's approval.
_Avoid_: Security deposit, ordinary rent prepayment

**Rent prepayment**:
Rent paid ahead of the month it covers during a tenancy, separate from the notice-period advance.
_Avoid_: Notice-period advance, security deposit

**Notice period**:
The agreed time between notice of departure and the end of a tenancy.
_Avoid_: Notice-period advance when referring to the length of time

**Due date**:
The date by which a charge is expected to be paid, independent of when payment is actually received.
_Avoid_: Payment date

**Payment date**:
The date money is actually received from a tenant.
_Avoid_: Due date

**Waiver**:
An amount the landlord explicitly agrees the tenant no longer needs to pay.
_Avoid_: Payment when referring to an amount forgiven without receiving money

**Main meter**:
The prepaid electricity meter supplying power shared by multiple tenant households.
_Avoid_: Submeter when referring to the shared supply meter

**Submeter**:
A meter measuring the electricity consumed by an individual household supplied through the main meter.
_Avoid_: Main meter when referring to a household's consumption meter

**Recharge contribution**:
Money a household pays to recharge the shared main prepaid meter, recorded with its date and contributing household. It is separate from that household's measured electricity cost.
_Avoid_: Electricity charge when referring to money contributed to the meter

**Electricity charge**:
The cost of a household's measured consumption over a period, calculated from the change in submeter reading multiplied by the applicable per-kWh rate.
_Avoid_: Recharge contribution when referring to consumption cost

**Electricity credit**:
The unused amount of a household's recharge contributions after covering its electricity charges, carried forward toward future electricity costs.
_Avoid_: Rent prepayment, notice-period advance

**Departure settlement**:
The landlord-reviewed reconciliation of a tenancy's outstanding charges, remaining advance, refunds, and approved adjustments when the tenant leaves.
_Avoid_: Payment when referring to the entire reconciliation
