# Notice and departure settlement

Owner: src/features/tenancies coordinates Ledger and Electricity. Read [Tenancies](../domains/tenancies.md) and relevant financial domain guides.

## Purpose and review

Explain and record the end of a tenancy without losing history or disguising cash movements.

Notice captures actual notice date, independently agreed duration and planned departure date. Review final charges/partial month, older dues, existing payments and remaining held advance. Show proposed approved advance application before recording it.

Present shortage and possible refund separately. Collection invokes the common payment flow; owner waiver preserves original charge plus reason/date/authority. Actual refund entry is not assumed from a negative calculated balance. Electricity credit needs its separately agreed disposition.

Final review shows each recorded outcome and remaining obligations before Confirm departure. Unit availability updates through the approved close operation; identity/documents and prior financial history remain.

## State and role coverage

A draft review is not a closed tenancy. Changed source records can require re-review; conflict and pending local saves remain explicit. Helpers cannot silently waive money. Keep all important values on the review screen, with a consistent Back/Next pattern and no timed confirmations.

Acceptance: A02, A09–A11, A17. Example: final rent 22,000 − held advance applied 20,000 = 2,000 remaining. Receiving 2,000 and waiving 2,000 are different histories.

Short notice, unused advance, actual refunds, final electricity credit and closure with collectible debt remain open. Build independent review portions without inventing the final eligibility rules.
